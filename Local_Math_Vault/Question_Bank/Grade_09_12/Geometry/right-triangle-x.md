In the right-angled triangle $ABC$ with $\angle B = 90\degree$, $AB = 4\text{ cm}$ and $BC = 3\text{ cm}$, find $x = \angle A$.

Choices:

- A. $\tan^{-1}(3/4)$
- B. $\tan^{-1}(4/3)$
- C. $36.87\degree$

Answer: $x = \tan^{-1}(3/4) \approx 36.87\degree$
