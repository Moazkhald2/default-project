# Freelance Pricing for Math Tutors

Skill for setting rates, structuring packages, and managing pricing as a math tutor.

## When to Use

- User asks "how much should I charge for tutoring"
- User wants to structure pricing packages
- User needs help raising rates
- User wants to understand payment models
- User is starting a tutoring business

## Rate Setting

### Rate Models

| Model | How It Works | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Hourly | Pay per session hour | Standard private tutoring |
| Package | Buy 5/10 sessions upfront | Predictable income, commitment |
| Subscription | Monthly fee for X sessions/week | Recurring revenue |
| Tiered | Different rates for different levels | Serving multiple markets |
| Value-based | Price based on outcome/competition | Premium positioning |

### Hourly Rate Calculator

```
Target annual income:  $_________
Working weeks/year:    48 (4 weeks off)
Hours/week tutoring:   15 (not driving/marketing)
Hours/year:            720

Rate = Target income / 720 hours
       + prep time markup (20%)
       + travel time markup (if applicable)
       + platform commission (if applicable)

Example:
Target: $50,000/year
720 hours × 1.2 (prep time) = 864 hours
Base rate: $50,000 / 864 = $58/hour
Round to: $60/hour
```

### Factors That Increase Rate

- Advanced degree (Master's +$10/hr, PhD +$20/hr)
- Teaching certification (+$10-15/hr)
- Specialization (calculus, AP, SAT: +$15-25/hr)
- Test prep expertise (SAT/ACT/AP: +$20-30/hr)
- Years of experience (+$3-5/hr per year)
- In-person (add travel time + gas)
- Demand season (finals week, SAT season: +20%)

## Package Pricing

### Package structures

**Per-session packages:**
```
Single session: $65
5 sessions:     $300 ($60/session — save $25)
10 sessions:    $550 ($55/session — save $100)
20 sessions:    $1,000 ($50/session — save $300)
```

**Monthly subscription:**
```
Basic:   4 sessions/month — $240 ($60/session)
Standard: 8 sessions/month — $440 ($55/session)
Premium:  12 sessions/month — $600 ($50/session)
```

**Exam prep intensive:**
```
SAT/ACT Premier (10 sessions + 3 practice tests): $800
AP Exam Bootcamp (6 sessions): $420
Finals Cram (4 sessions in 2 weeks): $280
```

### Pricing psychology tips
- Package pricing should offer 10-20% savings over per-session
- Subscriptions create commitment and reduce no-shows
- Higher packages should have higher total value but better per-session rate
- Use $X99 pricing ($49, $99, $199)
- Offer a "first session free" or discounted trial

## Discounts

| Discount Type | Amount | When to Offer |
|--------------|--------|---------------|
| Referral | 1 free session per referred client | Existing clients |
| Sibling | 10-15% off each sibling | Multiple students |
| Early-bird | 15% off if book semester in advance | Before term starts |
| Off-peak | 10% off for daytime slots | Low-demand hours |
| Group | 20-30% off per student for groups of 3+ | Group sessions |
| Pre-paid | 5% off for full semester payment upfront | Cash flow certainty |

## Payment Terms

### Options

| Term | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|
| Pay-per-session | Flexible, low commitment | Inconsistent income |
| Weekly billing | Regular income | More admin |
| Monthly billing | Predictable | Clients may forget |
| Upfront package | Best cash flow | Higher price objection |
| Retainer | Steady income | Needs high trust clients |

### Recommended terms

**New clients:** Pay-per-session or small package (5 sessions)
**Established clients:** Monthly billing or larger packages (10-20 sessions)
**School contracts:** Net-30 invoice

**Payment methods:**
- Venmo / Zelle / Cash App (instant, no fees)
- PayPal (+2.9% fee — pass on or absorb)
- Stripe / Square (professional invoicing)
- Bank transfer (for larger contracts)
- Check (declining, but some parents prefer)

**Late payment policy:**
- Payment due at time of session (pay-per-session)
- Payment due by 1st of month (subscriptions)
- Late fee: $10/day or 5% after 7 days (state this in contract)
- Suspension: No sessions if balance overdue > 2 weeks

## Raising Rates

### When to raise rates

| Trigger | Increase |
|---------|----------|
| Annual (every 12 months) | $5-10/hr |
| New certification/degree | $10-15/hr |
| Demand exceeds capacity | $10-20/hr |
| Inflation adjustment | 5-10% |
| Moving to new market | Match local rates |

### How to raise rates without losing clients

1. **Give notice**: 4-6 weeks advanced notice
2. **Grandfather loyal clients**: Keep old rate for existing clients for 1-3 months
3. **Explain the value**: "I've completed advanced training and updated my curriculum"
4. **Offer package lock-in**: "Sign a 20-session package at current rates"
5. **Increase in small increments**: $5 at a time is easier to swallow than $20

### Rate increase email template
```
Subject: Important update about tutoring rates

Hi [Parent Name],

I've loved working with [Student Name] this year and am proud of the
progress we've made — [specific achievement].

Starting [date], I'll be adjusting my rate from $[old] to $[new]/hour.
This reflects my continued professional development, updated materials,
and the personalized support I provide each student.

To make this transition smooth, I'm offering:
- Lock in current rates by purchasing a 10-session package before [date]
- Current students receive the old rate for one additional month

Thank you for your understanding and continued trust.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Contracts

### Essential clauses
- **Scope**: Subject areas, grade level, specific goals
- **Schedule**: Recurring session day/time
- **Rate**: Current rate and any scheduled increases
- **Payment terms**: Due date, methods, late fees
- **Cancellation**: Notice period (24-48 hrs), make-up policy
- **Refunds**: Pro-rated for prepaid packages
- **Termination**: Either party can terminate with [X] days notice
- **Liability**: Tutor not liable for student outcomes

### Cancellation policy templates

**Strict (recommended for busy tutors):**
- 24 hours notice required
- Less than 24 hours: session is charged in full
- Emergency/illness: waived with notice
- Tutor cancels: make-up session or credit

**Standard:**
- 24 hours notice required
- Less than 24 hours: 50% charge
- 2 free late cancellations per semester

**Flexible (new tutors building clientele):**
- 12 hours notice required
- Less than 12 hours: 50% charge
- Credit always offered, never cash penalty

## Tax Considerations

### Tracking income
- Keep a spreadsheet or use QuickBooks Self-Employed
- Record: date, client, hours, rate, total, payment method
- Send 1099 forms if using a platform (or track for yourself)

### Common deductions for tutors
- Home office space (dedicated area for lesson prep)
- Internet and phone (percentage used for business)
- Educational materials (books, software, subscriptions)
- Technology (laptop, tablet, calculator — partial if mixed use)
- Travel mileage (if driving to students: $0.655/mile in 2024)
- Professional development (courses, conferences)
- Marketing (website, business cards, ads)

### Quarterly estimated taxes
- If earning > $1,000/year as independent contractor
- Pay estimated tax quarterly (April 15, June 15, Sept 15, Jan 15)
- Set aside 25-30% of each payment for taxes
- Use IRS Form 1040-ES
