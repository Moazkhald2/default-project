# Marketing Content for Math Tutors

Skill for creating social media posts, emails, blog content, and promotional materials.

## When to Use

- User wants to attract new tutoring clients
- User needs social media content ideas
- User wants to build an email list
- User is creating a website or portfolio
- User wants to showcase student results

## Brand Voice

### Voice characteristics

| Quality | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Approachable | Not intimidating, warm | "Struggling with algebra? You're not alone — and we can fix it together." |
| Expert | Confident, knowledgeable | "Here's why your child keeps making the same mistake on the SAT math section." |
| Encouraging | Growth mindset focus | "Math isn't about being 'good at it' — it's about building understanding one step at a time." |
| Clear | Simple language, no jargon | "Think of it like a balance scale: whatever you do to one side, do to the other." |

### Tone by platform

| Platform | Tone | Content Style |
|----------|------|--------------|
| Instagram | Visual, friendly, short | Tips, problems, student wins, reels |
| TikTok | Fun, fast-paced, trendy | Quick tips, common mistakes, challenges |
| LinkedIn | Professional, expertise | Long-form tips, education discussion, credibility |
| Facebook | Community, conversational | Group posts, local community, parent-focused |
| Twitter/X | Quick, timely, threads | Tips, resources, quick threads |

## Blog / Newsletter Topics

### Topic categories

**Study skills (broad appeal):**
- "How to Study for a Math Test: A 7-Day Plan"
- "5 Note-Taking Methods That Actually Work for Math"
- "Why Practice Problems Aren't Enough (And What to Do Instead)"
- "Math Anxiety: What It Is and How to Overcome It"

**Exam prep (high urgency):**
- "SAT Math vs ACT Math: Which Is Easier?"
- "AP Calculus BC: What You Need to Know Before Taking the Exam"
- "Last-Minute Finals Study Guide: Algebra 2"
- "How to Get a 5 on the AP Statistics Exam"

**Parent-focused (the buyer):**
- "Is Your Child Falling Behind in Math? 7 Warning Signs"
- "What to Do When Your Child Says 'I'm Just Not Good at Math'"
- "Online Tutoring vs In-Person: Which Is Better for Your Child?"
- "How Much Should You Spend on Math Tutoring?"

**Seasonal (timely):**
- "Back-to-School Math Prep: What to Review Before September"
- "Summer Slide: How to Keep Math Skills Sharp During Break"
- "Finals Are Coming: How to Prepare in 4 Weeks"
- "New Year, New Math Goals: Setting Your Child Up for Success"

### Blog post structure

```
Title: [Compelling, keyword-rich title]

Opening hook (1-2 paragraphs)
- Problem or pain point
- Relatable scenario
- Statistic or surprising fact

Body (3-5 sections)
- Break down the solution
- Use subheadings
- Include examples
- Add visuals (images, graphs)

Practical tips (actionable)
- Step-by-step instructions
- Checklist or template

Call to action (CTA)
- "Book a free consultation"
- "Download the study guide"
- "Share this with a friend"
```

## Testimonials

### How to collect testimonials

**Ask at the right time:**
- After a grade improvement
- After a positive session
- After a successful exam
- When a student says something complimentary

**Ask specific questions:**
- "What was the biggest challenge before tutoring?"
- "What specific results did you see?"
- "What did you like most about working with me?"
- "Would you recommend me? What would you say?"

**Testimonial format:**
```
"Before working with [Name], I was really struggling with
calculus. After 3 months of weekly sessions, I got an A on
my final exam and actually understand the concepts now.
[Name] explains things in a way that just clicks."

— [Student Name], [Grade/Course]
[optional: photo, grade improvement]
```

- Website (dedicated testimonials page)
- Instagram highlights
- Facebook page
- Google Business profile
- LinkedIn recommendations
- Flyers and brochures

## Portfolio / Case Studies

### Case study format (anonymous)

```
# Case Study: From C-Student to A in Algebra 2

## The Challenge
Student was a 10th grader consistently earning C's in Algebra 2.
Parent reported low confidence and homework avoidance.

## Assessment
- Gaps in Algebra 1 fundamentals (especially factoring)
- Test anxiety affecting performance
- Weak homework habits (rushing, not checking work)

## Approach
1. Spent 4 sessions reinforcing Algebra 1 gaps
2. Introduced test-taking strategies
3. Created structured homework routine

## Results
- Grade improved from C- to A- in one semester
- Student reports feeling "confident going into tests"
- Homework time reduced from 2hrs to 45min

## Timeline
12 weeks, 2 sessions per week
```

## SEO for Local Tutoring

### Keywords to target

**Primary (high-intent):**
- "math tutor [city name]"
- "[city name] math tutoring"
- "algebra tutor near me"
- "online math tutor"

**Secondary (topic-specific):**
- "calculus tutor [city]"
- "SAT math prep [city]"
- "middle school math help"
- "high school geometry tutor"

**Long-tail (low competition):**
- "best math tutor for teenagers in [city/suburb]"
- "how to find a good algebra tutor online"
- "math tutoring for struggling high school students"
- "affordable math help for college students"

### SEO best practices
- Include location in page titles and headings
- Create Google Business profile (with reviews)
- Get listed on tutoring directories (Wyzant, TutorMe, Care.com)
- Blog regularly with relevant topics
- Encourage Google reviews from clients
